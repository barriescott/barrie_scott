About me

A journey built on ambition.

I am Alhaji Mamadu Allieu Barrie, known as Barrie Scott. Born in Bo City, Sierra Leone, I believe success comes from learning, staying determined and using what you know to make a positive difference.

My foundation

My education includes university studies and practical vocational training. I trained in arc welding at the Sierra Leone Opportunities Industrialisation Centres (SLOIC), developing skills in metalwork and a commitment to my craft.

My purpose

My dream is to succeed in life and become one of the best welders Sierra Leone has produced. I also want to build my fashion business, grow as a motivational speaker and encourage young Africans to believe in their potential.
